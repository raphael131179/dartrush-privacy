DartRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Summary

DartRush does not ask for an account and does not collect personal data for its own purposes. The game is free and financed by advertising; the advertising provider processes some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, best distance, and local leaderboard are stored only on your device using the standard iOS preferences system. They never leave your device through the app and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

DartRush shows advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). To deliver and measure ads, Google may process device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; details are described in Google's privacy information for advertising and its partner policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, DartRush asks for your consent before any ads are requested, using Google's certified consent dialog. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time via Ad privacy settings on the app's home screen. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

On iOS, the app additionally asks whether it may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the Advertising Identifier is not used and ads are not personalised. The game is fully playable in either case.

Watching a rewarded video is always optional and only ever offered as a choice; declining never blocks any part of the game.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on the worldwide leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple Inc. and is subject to Apple's privacy policy. Signing in is optional; the game is fully playable without it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

You can withdraw advertising consent at any time in the app, reset the Advertising Identifier under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and delete all locally stored data by removing the app. For questions about data Google processes, please refer to Google's privacy policy linked above.
